Basic eligibility criteria

👤 Who may qualify?

  • Age: 21–60 years
  • Salaried + good credit score: income above ₹15,000/month
  • Salaried + zero credit score: income above ₹25,000/month
  • Self-employed + good credit score: income above ₹30,000/month
  • Self-employed + zero credit history: not allowed as per provided material

📄 Documents / verification

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar
  • Income proof where required
  • Video KYC / other verification
  • Provided material states customer should be within 50 km of Aadhaar or communication address during Video KYC.
